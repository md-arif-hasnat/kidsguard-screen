# Family Management \u0026 Multi-Parent Collaboration

KidsGuard supports collaborative monitoring through a role-based access control (RBAC) system.

## Roles \u0026 Permissions

| Permission | Owner | Parent | Guardian | Viewer |

## Member Management

### Family Owner
The user who created the family. Has absolute control over all settings and members.

### Parent
Full administrative access to child safety features, but cannot manage other adult members.

### Guardian
Limited access, typically for grandparents or babysitters. Can see live location and receive SOS alerts, but cannot change safety rules.

### Viewer
Read-only access. Useful for older siblings or temporary monitors.

## Child Assignment
Guardians can be assigned to specific children. A guardian assigned to "Emma" will only seeEmma's data and receive Emma's alerts.

## Emergency Contacts
A shared list of emergency contacts (Family, Neighbors, Police, Doctor) that are synced across all parent/guardian dashboards.

## Administrative Activity Log
All major changes (role updates, invites, setting changes) are logged in the family activity feed for transparency between parents.
