# KidsGuard Product Requirements

## Product Name

KidsGuard

# Vision

KidsGuard is a modern Family Safety Platform.

Its mission is to help parents keep their children safe while respecting privacy and platform rules.

KidsGuard is NOT a spyware application.

---

# Core Philosophy

Protect families.

Respect privacy.

Build trust.

Provide transparency.

Never secretly spy on users.
