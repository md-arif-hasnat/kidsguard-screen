# KidsGuard PWA Install Guide

KidsGuard Parent Dashboard is a Progressive Web App (PWA), meaning you can install it on your mobile device without using the App Store or Play Store.

## Benefits of Installing
- **Full Screen**: No browser address bars or navigation buttons.
- **Home Screen Access**: Launch KidsGuard directly from your home screen like any other app.
- **Fast Access**: No need to type the URL every time.
- **Background Persistence**: Better session management on mobile.

## How to Install

### iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)
1. Open **Safari** and go to the KidsGuard Dashboard.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an up arrow) at the bottom (iPhone) or top (iPad) of the screen.
3. Scroll down the share menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. KidsGuard will now appear on your Home Screen.

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open **Chrome** and go to the KidsGuard Dashboard.
2. Tap the **Menu** icon (three vertical dots ⋮) in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm installation.

### Desktop (Chrome / Edge)
1. Open the dashboard in your browser.
2. Look for the **"Install"** icon in the address bar (right side).
3. Click it and select **"Install"**.

## Push Notifications
PWAs on iOS 16.4+ and Android support standard push notifications. You may be prompted to allow notifications after adding the app to your Home Screen.

## Limitations
- Native hardware access (like advanced biometric APIs) may be restricted compared to the native KidsGuard child app.
- App must be added to Home Screen to behave as a standalone application.
