# KidsGuard Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Last Updated: October 2023**

## 1. Introduction
KidsGuard ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your family's privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our child protection platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information from Parents
- Email address and password for account management.
- Family name and role information.
- Log of administrative actions (Audit Logs).

### 2.2 Information from Child Devices
- **Location Data**: Real-time GPS coordinates to provide tracking and safe zone alerts.
- **App Usage**: Statistics on which apps are used and for how long.
- **Web Activity**: URLs visited to provide content filtering and safe search.
- **Device Status**: Battery level, online status, and OS version.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To allow parents to monitor and protect their children.
- To trigger automated safety rules (Protection Modes).
- To send push notifications for critical events (SOS, Safe Zones).
- To improve our AI safety insights.

## 4. Data Sharing & Security
- We **never** sell your data to third parties.
- Data is only shared with authorized family members based on the roles defined by the Family Owner.
- All data is encrypted in transit and stored securely using Google Firebase.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
- You can export all your data at any time via the Security Dashboard.
- You can request account deletion, which permanently removes all profile and child telemetry.

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
KidsGuard is designed for parents to monitor their own children. We require parental consent during the pairing process and do not collect data from children without a link to a parent account.
